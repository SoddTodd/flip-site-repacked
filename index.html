November, 2026 - The sun is clocking out early, the city is freezing, and we’re hijacking the dark. On December 21, Germany’s official Short Film Day (Kurzfilmtag), flip drops its first dose of unfiltered, homebrewed animation directly onto the big screen at Brotfabrik. No polite corporate filler, no slick studio sheen: just raw frames, ink stains, scratched celluloid, melted pixels, and stubborn DIY visionaries telling stories at 24 frames per second. Come hide from the solstice and watch things move.
