Submissions

open call: rolling submissions

flip has no deadlines, no submission fees, and no gatekeeping. We curate ongoing programmes across Berlin’s independent cinema screens as dates and venues confirm.

Whether your work is freshly rendered, scribbled on loose paper, or pulled from a dusty hard drive, send us a link. If selected for an upcoming screening, we’ll reach out to request a high-res projection file.
